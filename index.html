201 Pool & Closed Joker Rules

Official Indian Rummy Tournament Guidelines

1. The Closed Joker & First Life Rule

The cut Wild Joker remains strictly locked and hidden 🔒 from each player until they hold a valid Pure Sequence (First Life) in their hand. Once you group at least 3 consecutive cards of the same suit without jokers, the vault opens and highlights your wild jokers in gold!

2. 201 Elimination Pool

Points accumulate each round. As soon as a friend reaches 201 or more points, they are permanently Eliminated. The last remaining player wins the tournament!

3. First Drop & Middle Drop

  • First Drop (20 pts): Dropping on your turn before drawing any card in the round.
  • Middle Drop (40 pts): Dropping on your turn before drawing after having played one or more turns.

4. Valid Declaration & Deadwood

To declare: You need 13 cards arranged with at least 1 Pure Sequence + 1 Second Sequence (pure or with jokers), plus valid sets. Face cards and Aces = 10 pts, numbers = face value. Maximum penalty cap is 80 points. Wrong declaration = 80 penalty points.
